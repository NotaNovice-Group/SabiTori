# Two-minute demo video: shot list

For the Mozilla application and anyone who asks "show me". One take on a real Android phone, screen recording plus
a second camera on the hands if you have one. Everything shown below exists in the app today; do not show or say
anything else. Speak slowly; add captions in English and French.

**Ready-made one-minute screen demo.** `apps/app/bench/demo/record.mjs` records the built app in a phone frame
(pasted French bill explained in English, scam SMS warned first, photo of a paper bill read on the device) with
captions and the spoken words as subtitles; no soundtrack, for a voice-over. Recorded in desktop Chromium at phone
size, not on a phone; the photo-reading wait is cut by about 3 s. Say both if you use it. The phone recording below
is still the stronger proof (airplane mode visible, real voice).

**Before recording**

- Install the latest APK (`docs/release.md`). French voice bundled. Turn on **airplane mode** and leave it on: the
  status bar shows it the whole time, which is the point.
- Settings: language French, "Listen first" **on** (so each button says its name before it acts).
- Print the ENEO example ("Practice with an example", the electricity bill) or use a letter of your own that holds no one's private data. Never
  film a real person's message without their written consent.
- Clear history (Settings, "Delete all my messages") so nothing private is on screen.

| 0:00–0:15 | Your face, or the phone on a table | [OWNER: one or two sentences: who you are, the person you saw struggle with an official message, why it matters to you] |

| 0:50–1:15 | Home → camera → photograph the printed bill; the card appears; tap Listen | "A bill on paper. The phone reads it and speaks the card: who sent it, how much, by when, who to call." |
| 1:15–1:30 | Tap the amount on the card: the sheet shows it highlighted in the message | "Every number comes from the message itself. Tap it and you see exactly where." |
| 1:30–1:40 | "Remind me" on the bill card | "A reminder before the deadline." |
| 1:40–1:50 | Tap a button once (it says its name), tap again (it acts) | "Each button says its name first, so you can use it without reading." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Back to you, or the About screen | "Open source, offline, and built so communities can add their own languages. [OWNER: what you ask for: the pilot, the partner, the first Cameroonian language.]" |

**Do not say:** that it has users, partners or a pilot unless that is true on the day; that a message is "safe"
(the app never says so); that it speaks Pidgin, Duala or any local language yet (French and English only today);
accuracy numbers other than those in the README, with their conditions.

**After recording:** upload unlisted, put the link in the application form and in `README.md`, and keep the raw
file: the pilot partner may want to use it in their own language.
