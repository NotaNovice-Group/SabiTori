# Sustainability: how SabiTori is meant to last

SabiTori is a public-interest project. This page says how we intend to keep it running without
selling anything. It is a plan: none of the funding below exists yet, and no partner has signed.

## What does not change

- **The app is free, for everyone, forever.** No price, no paid tier, no in-app purchases, no ads.
- **No data business.** Messages and photos never leave the phone (`docs/privacy.md`); we do not
  sell, rent or share data, and there is none to sell.
- **Open by default.** Code is MPL-2.0; language packs are CC-BY-SA-4.0 unless their community
  chooses another licence (`packs/LICENSE.md`); the evaluation data and training recipe are public.
  Anyone may fork it.
- **Communities own their languages.** Recordings, packs and any voice trained on them belong to the
  language community that made them, which also chooses their licence.

## Where the money is meant to come from (non-commercial)

1. **Grants** from foundations and public programmes that fund open, public-interest technology,
   digital inclusion, accessibility and language preservation.
2. **Programme partnerships.** Non-governmental organisations and public programmes in public health,
   financial inclusion, education, literacy and disability rights already need people to understand
   their messages (vaccination calls, mobile-money warnings, school notices). A partner can fund the
   recording of a language its programme needs; the result is published as an open pack that everyone
   can use. Partners do not buy a product, get exclusive rights, or receive any user data.
3. **Community contributions** of time and skill: translation, recording, review, testing, and
   language committees or literacy programmes that take care of their language's pack.
4. **A charitable home.** The project is meant to be held by a non-profit structure (a charitable
   association or company in Germany, or a fiscal sponsor) that can receive grants and steward the
   community packs: [OWNER: publisher — individual founder or charitable vehicle].

## What we will not do

- Charge people, organisations or governments for the app or for a "premium" version.
- Sell or license user data, usage data or message content (we do not collect any).
- Show ads or sponsored content, or let a partner change what a card says.
- Give anyone exclusive rights to a community's language pack or voice.
